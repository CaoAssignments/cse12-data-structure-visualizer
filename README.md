# CSE 12 Data Structure Visualizer

Step-by-step animations of the data structures from the CSE 12 programming assignments, with a small
study companion named Pawie. Open `index.html`, the site's front page.

## There are no accounts

Nothing asks for a name, email or password. Pawie's level, XP and outfits are kept in your own browser.
Use **Save to file** in the Pawie panel to keep a copy, and **Load from file** to bring Pawie to another
device or back after clearing your browser data.

## What is collected

The site sends anonymous usage events: which structure and method you ran, whether you answered a quiz
question correctly, which Pawie buttons you used, and Pawie's XP, level and streak. They are tied to a random
id stored in your browser, never to you. No names, emails, code or typed text are sent. Turn on "Do Not
Track" in your browser and nothing is sent at all.
